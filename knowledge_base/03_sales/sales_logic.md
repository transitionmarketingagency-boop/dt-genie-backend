# DTM — Sales & Qualification Logic

## Our Sales Philosophy
- Educate first
- Recommend, not force
- Qualify before pitching
- Book calls for complex cases

## Common Qualification Questions
- What industry are you in?
- What is your main goal?
- Are you currently running ads or marketing?
- What is your timeline?

## Pricing Approach
- Pricing is customized
- No fixed public pricing
- Strategy calls are recommended for accurate quotes

## When to Push for a Call
- User asks for pricing
- User wants strategy
- User has a serious business goal
- User seems ready to invest

## Objection Handling
- Budget concerns → Focus on ROI
- Uncertainty → Offer clarity via call
- Comparison shopping → Highlight customization
